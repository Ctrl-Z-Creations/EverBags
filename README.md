# EverBags

One-window, categorized bags and bank for **World of Warcraft: Forever**.

By [Ctrl+Z Creations](https://github.com/Ctrl-Z-Creations).

## Install

Get it from CurseForge or Wago, or download the zip from [Releases](https://github.com/Ctrl-Z-Creations/EverBags/releases) and unzip it into your WoW: Forever `Interface\AddOns` folder.

## Features

**One window, organized**
- Every bag in a single window, with optional sections: Equipment, Consumables, Trade Goods, Recipes, Quest, Keys, Misc and Junk
- Item level on gear, a coin icon on sellable junk, and free slots collapsed into one
- Keyring shown as its own Keys section
- Adjustable columns, scale and window titles, plus an optional textured background

**Bank replacement**
- Matching bank window for Forever's bank tabs
- **Deposit** button moves Trade Goods (or whatever you've searched for) to the bank in one click

**Smart search**
- Search by name or keyword: `food`, `boe`, `soulbound`, `new`, quality, slot or section names
- Combine terms: `green cloak`, use commas for "or" (`potion, food`), exclude with a minus (`potion -healing`)
- Filter by item level: `ilvl>50`, `ilvl<30`

**Stacking and protection**
- One click merges partial stacks
- Mark items as **Keep** to lock them: Stack and Deposit leave them alone, and they're never suggested as drops

**Item values**
- See what each section, and your whole inventory, is worth
- Uses vendor prices, or Auctionator prices when those are higher
- When your bags are nearly full, EverBags highlights the best items to drop

**Farming summary**
- A running tally of what you've looted this session, items and gold, shown in the footer ("+38 items · +5g 32s")
- Counts only loot, not vendor, mail or auction house income

**Alt inventory**
- Browse any of your characters' bags, bank and gold from any character
- Item tooltips show which of your alts hold that item

## Commands

`/everbags` or `/eb` opens settings.
